# Problem Statement

## Track 4 – UnlockED
### PS 3: Hyper-Local Job Matching for Informal & Semi-Skilled Workers

## 1. Background

Informal and semi-skilled workers play an important role in the economy. They provide essential services across construction, transportation, maintenance, household services, retail, and other sectors.

However, many workers still depend on personal contacts, local references, and informal networks to find employment.

## 2. Key Problems

1. **Limited Job Accessibility:** Workers struggle to discover suitable employment opportunities in nearby locations.

2. **Lack of Verified Opportunities:** Workers may find it difficult to identify trustworthy employers and genuine job openings.

3. **Unorganized Recruitment:** Employers often face challenges finding suitable and available workers quickly.

4. **Communication Barriers:** Language and digital literacy limitations can make traditional job platforms difficult to use.

5. **Lack of Trust:** The absence of proper ratings, reviews, and verification mechanisms creates uncertainty between workers and employers.

6. **Workplace Safety Concerns:** Workers may lack accessible safety mechanisms and emergency support.

7. **Limited Employment Management:** Workers often lack a centralized way to manage their job applications, work history, and employment activities.

## 3. Need for a Solution

There is a need for an accessible, reliable, and technology-driven platform that connects informal and semi-skilled workers with nearby employment opportunities.

The solution should simplify job discovery, improve communication, establish trust, and provide safety support for workers.

## 4. Our Objective

WorkSync AI aims to bridge the gap between local workers and employers through AI-powered job matching, digital employment management, verification mechanisms, and safety-oriented features.

**Our Vision:** Making local employment more accessible, transparent, reliable, and safer for everyone.
