# WorkSync AI – Safety & Trust Framework

## 1. Introduction

Worker safety and trust are essential components of WorkSync AI.

The platform proposes a dedicated safety ecosystem designed to promote secure employment interactions between workers and employers.

## 2. Safety Features

### 1. SOS Emergency Button

Provides workers with an emergency assistance mechanism that can be accessed during unsafe situations.

Proposed capabilities:
- Quick access to emergency assistance.
- Sharing relevant emergency information.
- Supporting communication with designated emergency contacts.

### 2. Two-Way Verification

Supports verification of both workers and employers to improve platform credibility and reduce identity-related risks.

### 3. Safe Job Location Sharing

Allows relevant job-location information to be shared with designated contacts to improve awareness and safety.

### 4. AI Scam Detection

Aims to identify suspicious job postings and potentially fraudulent employment activities.

### 5. Two-Way Safety Reporting

Allows both workers and employers to report inappropriate behaviour, suspicious activities, or safety-related concerns.

### 6. Job-Specific Safety Checklist

Provides safety reminders based on the nature of the job.

Examples:
- Construction work.
- Electrical maintenance.
- Cleaning services.
- Delivery services.

### 7. SafeJob Assessment

Provides a job-related safety assessment using available job information to help users make informed decisions.

## 3. Trust and Reputation Features

### Two-Way Ratings

Allows workers and employers to rate each other after completed work.

### Verified Reviews

Supports feedback associated with recorded job interactions.

### TrustScore

A proposed reputation indicator based on relevant platform activity and trust-related information.

### Repeat Hire Indicator

Highlights previous working relationships between workers and employers.

### Payment Reliability

Supports transparency regarding recorded payment history and payment-related feedback.

### Skill Endorsements

Allows employers to recognize workers' demonstrated skills.

### Dispute Resolution

Provides a structured process for reporting and addressing employment-related disagreements.

## 4. Safety Framework Objectives

- Promote safer employment interactions.
- Improve identity and profile credibility.
- Reduce suspicious job activities.
- Encourage transparent communication.
- Improve accountability.
- Strengthen worker confidence.

## 5. Privacy and Responsible Design

The platform aims to follow privacy-conscious practices.

- Collect only necessary user information.
- Restrict access to sensitive information.
- Protect user authentication details.
- Obtain appropriate consent for location sharing.
- Handle emergency information responsibly.

## 6. Future Enhancements

Potential improvements include:
- More advanced suspicious activity detection.
- Additional regional-language safety assistance.
- Improved emergency notification mechanisms.
- Stronger verification integrations.

**Implementation Note:** These are proposed safety and trust capabilities. Their effectiveness will depend on implementation, verification, testing, and appropriate external integrations.
