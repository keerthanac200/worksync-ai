# WorkSync AI – System Workflow

## 1. Introduction

WorkSync AI connects informal and semi-skilled workers with employers through a hyper-local employment platform.

The platform provides separate workflows for workers and employers.

# 2. Worker Workflow

### Step 1: Registration
Workers create an account and provide their basic details.

### Step 2: Profile Creation
Workers add their skills, experience, preferred job types, availability, and location.

### Step 3: Profile Verification
Workers can complete the available verification process to improve profile credibility.

### Step 4: Job Discovery
Workers explore nearby job opportunities based on their location and preferences.

### Step 5: AI Job Matching
The AI matching component analyzes relevant information such as skills, location, experience, and availability to recommend suitable jobs.

### Step 6: Job Application
Workers select suitable opportunities and submit applications.

### Step 7: Employer Interaction
Workers receive application updates and communicate with employers.

### Step 8: Job Completion
After completing work, workers can maintain employment records and track payment status.

### Step 9: Ratings and Reviews
Workers and employers can provide feedback about their working experience.

### Step 10: Safety Support
Workers can access safety-related features, including the SOS Emergency Button.

# 3. Employer Workflow

### Step 1: Registration
Employers create an account and provide their details.

### Step 2: Employer Verification
Employers can complete the available verification process.

### Step 3: Job Posting
Employers publish job opportunities with details such as:
- Job description.
- Required skills.
- Work location.
- Wages.
- Working hours.
- Number of workers required.

### Step 4: Worker Discovery
Employers explore suitable worker profiles.

### Step 5: AI-Based Recommendations
The platform recommends potentially suitable workers based on job requirements.

### Step 6: Application Review
Employers review applications and worker profiles.

### Step 7: Worker Selection
Employers select suitable candidates and communicate with them.

### Step 8: Work Completion
Employers update the job status after work completion.

### Step 9: Ratings and Reviews
Employers provide feedback about workers and can receive feedback from them.
